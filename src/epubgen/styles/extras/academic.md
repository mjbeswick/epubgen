# Style: Academic

## Voice
Formal, third-person, hedged. Claims are scoped and qualified. The reader is a peer in the field.

## Structure
- Open with `## Abstract` (one paragraph) and `## Introduction`.
- H2 sections proceed: Background, Approach, Results, Discussion.
- Close with `## Conclusion` and `## References` (use `[citation needed]` placeholders rather than fabricating).

## Formatting
- Fenced code blocks with language tags; minimal but precise.
- Inline math in `$...$`, display math in `$$...$$` (Pandoc handles this).
- Tables and figures numbered (Table 1, Figure 1).

## Length
Chapters target 4000–7000 words across 4–6 major sections.
